Renewable energy / Feasibility2019–2020

Floating solar PV in Bangladesh

Assessing the feasibility of a 1 MW floating solar installation, combining energy modelling with deployment considerations.

System Advisor ModelSolar PVFeasibility study
Read project overview

Approach. Modelled a 1 MW floating solar PV system using SAM and evaluated technical, spatial, and environmental considerations for deployment in Bangladesh.

Modelled results. Estimated approximately 1.6 million kWh of annual generation and a 9.6-year payback period under the study assumptions. These are feasibility estimates, rather than measured operating results.

Engineering focus. Connecting energy yield, project economics, and site considerations to assess a renewable-energy concept.

Fluid dynamics / Wind energy2018–2019
03

Wind flow over building rooftops

Investigating how rooftop shape affects local wind conditions and the potential placement of wind turbines.

ANSYS FluentCFDGeometry comparison
Read project overview

Approach. Simulated airflow over dome, filleted, half-wedge, convergent, and tulip rooftop geometries. Compared velocity profiles and contours to examine the influence of building shape on wind flow.

Finding. The study identified dome and filleted profiles as providing enhanced wind-speed amplification over conventional designs in the simulated cases.

Engineering focus. Interpreting flow fields and comparing design alternatives for wind-energy applications.

Thermal analysis / Fluid flow2017–2018
04

Heat transfer in constricted channels

Exploring the relationship between channel geometry, flow behaviour, and convective heat transfer between parallel plates.

CFD modellingHeat transferParametric analysis
Read project overview

Approach. Developed CFD models with varying constriction sizes and quantities. Evaluated pressure, velocity, temperature, heat-transfer coefficient, and Nusselt number across the design cases.

Analysis. Examined flow acceleration, turbulence, and recirculation to understand their relationship with thermal performance.

Engineering focus. Using parametric studies to assess geometry-dependent flow and heat-transfer behaviour.
